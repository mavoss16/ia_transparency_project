## Write-ins

The 2020 files list write-ins by name like 2018, with three new forms:

- **Marked names**: "Dennis Bush (W)", "Deb Sharper (write-in)". `02` strips the mark and flags the name `write_in`.
- **"(Blank)"** as the write-in tally label.
- **Unmarked write-ins in one-county races**: Megan Srinivas got 64 write-ins in the HD 9 Democratic primary, which nobody filed for, and would have shown on her page as a lost primary. Every one-county primary name that the race's unnamed write-ins outpoll (37 rows, all 2018 and 2020; none in other years) was a write-in, so `02` flags them by rule.

Deb Scharper (SD 26 Dem) won the nomination as a write-in (marked in one county); she is `write_in` and her general row has no party, which is right.

### Label rules moved from the parser to `02`

`parsers.R`'s `clean_candidate()` now only squishes whitespace; `02` maps "Write-in" in any case, "Scattering", "Blank"/"(Blank)" and "No Name" to `Write-in` (`WRITE_IN_LABEL`). A label rule in the parser changes every election's fingerprint and forces a ~10-minute full parse; in `02` it costs seconds. Checked: the full parse after the move, with the old label rule applied, is identical (102,129 rows, same order) to the parse before it.

### Corrections file

First rows: "Camella Schultes" and "Camella Schuttes" → Carmella Schultes (2020 HD 10 Dem; three spellings across four counties left the race with no ballot candidate).
